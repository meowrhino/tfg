When I first saw the original cover of this song, I was a depressed teenager that dropped out of school and had pretty bad life choices made. I was ready to jump off a building somewhere and end things. I related to this song so much and it encapsulated everything I felt at that time. I was lonely, alone, and struggling to sort out how I feel. Then life got better slowly. I listened less and less to this song as I immersed myself in a lot of things and that loneliness was now a thing of the past. YT just randomly recommend me this out of nowhere and all I can say that it has been a long time. Now I'm getting married in a few months and have a child incoming in 3. I have a wonderful job and an absolutely amazing fiance. Idk whats the point of me commenting all this. I guess I can finally say that I'm no longer the error that I felt I was. Thank you for being a part of the toughest moments in my life kyaami.
