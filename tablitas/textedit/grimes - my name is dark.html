my name is dark - grimes


“My Name is Dark”, originally titled “That’s What the Drugs Are For”, was first heard as a small snippet for Apple’s “Behind the Mac” ad campaign. The track has since undergone many changes since that commercial snippet, now being a heavier nu-metal song. According to Grimes the song is about her insomnia.
